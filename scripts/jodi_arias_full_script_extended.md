## OUTRO

🔴 The next day she gave Flores a second story: two masked intruders had killed Travis while she escaped. Two years later she gave a third: she had killed him in self-defense. On May 8th, 2013, after a five-month trial watched around the world, a jury convicted Jodi Arias of first-degree murder. Two juries then failed to agree on whether she should die for it. On April 13th, 2015, Judge Sherry Stephens sentenced her to natural life in prison without the possibility of parole.

🔴 Travis Alexander was 30. He had worked his way out of a childhood his friends describe as very hard, and spent his last years telling rooms full of strangers that they could do the same. The people who found him were the people he had been expecting on a trip to Cancun that week.
