## SET UP

🔴 On Christmas morning 2019, 25-year-old Kevin Bacon did not come home to his family in Swartz Creek, Michigan. His car was found in a parking lot, his phone and clothes still inside it. In his messages, his family found a man who lived half an hour away, in rural Shiawassee County. The last messages were from Christmas Eve.

**@editor show the MSP report cover page (incident 035-0007899-19) and the line "missing since 12/24/2019".**

🔴 Just after midnight on December 28th, five officers approached a brick house on Tyrrell Road. Two of them crept across a frozen field first, to watch the windows. Through an upstairs window: a blue light, black leather gear, a chrome chain. Then the others pulled into the driveway and knocked. The man who opened the door was wearing a kilt.

🔴 He said he was home alone. He said he had never heard of anyone named Kevin Bacon. And when the trooper asked to come in and look around, he said:
