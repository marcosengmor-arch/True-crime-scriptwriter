SET UP

On Christmas morning 2019, 25-year-old Kevin Bacon did not come home to his family in Swartz Creek, Michigan. His car was found in a parking lot, his phone and clothes still inside it. In his messages, his family found a man who lived half an hour away, in rural Shiawassee County. The last messages were from Christmas Eve.

@editor show the MSP report cover page (incident 035-0007899-19) and the line "missing since 12/24/2019".

Just after midnight on December 28th, five officers approached a brick house on Tyrrell Road. Two of them crept across a frozen field first, to watch the windows. Through an upstairs window: a blue light, black leather gear, a chrome chain. Then the others pulled into the driveway and knocked. The man who opened the door was wearing a kilt.

He said he was home alone. He said he had never heard of anyone named Kevin Bacon. And when the trooper asked to come in and look around, he said:

At the far end of the basement, Trooper Viviano found a sliding barn door. Behind it, a second door, bolted from the outside and warm to the touch. Behind that, a hidden room with chains on the walls. And inside it, the young man they had come looking for.

Mark Latunski was handcuffed in his own hallway. As the cuffs went on, he exhaled and said, "You're making a mistake." Then he asked for a detective, so he could clear up the misunderstanding and go back inside his house. At 5:56 that morning, two detective sergeants sat down with him at the Corunna state police post. Before anything else, they needed his name. That was the first problem.

OUTRO

The cadaver dog found nothing on the property. The two men who had called 911 in October and November were never charged with anything, and neither was Latunski for what happened to them. In September 2022 he pleaded guilty to open murder and to mutilation of a body, with no deal from prosecutors. A judge then had to decide whether the killing was premeditated. In October he ruled that it was, calling it a cold calculation, and on December 15th, 2022, Mark Latunski was sentenced to life in prison without the possibility of parole.

Kevin Bacon was 25. He was a hairstylist and a student, and the people who loved him describe him as the person who made everyone else feel welcome. He went to meet a stranger on Christmas Eve and trusted him. That trust is the whole case.
